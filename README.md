# A flux plateau for site-disordered finite-capacity exclusion

Yitzchak Shmalo, The Hebrew University of Jerusalem.

Totally asymmetric exclusion on the integer lattice with capacity K at each
site, where site i releases particles at rate alpha_i and the rates are
independent and identically distributed. For every finite K and every
nonconstant rate law supported on [r,1] with essential infimum r > 0, the
hydrodynamic flux equals r M_K on the whole interval

    |rho - K/2| <= mu r M_K,   mu = 1/r - E[1/alpha_0],

where M_K is the maximum of the homogeneous rate-one flux, and r M_K is the
maximal flux. T. Seppäläinen, "Recent results and open problems on the
hydrodynamics of disordered asymmetric exclusion and zero-range processes",
Resenhas IME-USP 4 (1999) 1-15 (arXiv:math/9906194), Section 4, Problem 4,
asks whether this flux has a flat segment about K/2. For K = 1 the statement
is Theorem 1 of A. Sly, arXiv:1609.06589; Bahadoran and Bodineau (Electron.
J. Probab. 23 (2018), paper 44) treat dilute disorder and note that their
approach extends to K-exclusion.
